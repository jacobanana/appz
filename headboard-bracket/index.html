How to print it

  1. Lay the profile flat on the bed — no supports, and the layer lines run across the load instead of splitting it.
  2. PETG over PLA. PLA creeps under a constant load in a warm room and a bracket over your pillow is not the place to find that out.
  3. 4 perimeters. On a cantilever the perimeters carry the load, not the infill.
  4. Print one, hang it, load it with something heavier than the projector, leave it overnight. Then print the second.
  5. Line the saddle with adhesive felt — grips better, won't mark the wood.
