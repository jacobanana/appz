Geneva's network, breathing.

Every dot is a tpg stop. How big it is, is how many people got on there last month. Drag the year and watch the whole canton inhale and exhale.

Then press Rhythm — and the map dissolves. The stops re-arrange themselves not by where they are, but by the shape of their year. Geography evaporates; a school stop in Vernier lands next to a school stop in Carouge, and the lake shore drifts off on its own.
