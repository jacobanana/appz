Look up any tpg stop and see everything the open data holds on it — its whole counted history, every line that calls there, and how that mix shifted. Then scroll on and watch the network re-arrange itself by the shape of its year.

        The map that forgets where it is
        
          
        Same dots, two arrangements. Map puts each stop at its real coordinates.
             Rhythm throws the coordinates away and places each stop by how its ridership
             is spread across the year — so neighbours in this view are stops that breathe alike.

        Where this comes from
        
            Straight from the portal, nothing cached
            
      
            
      
            
        Every number on this page was fetched from the
        Explore v2.1 API
        when you opened it. The app reads each dataset's own field list first and uses the portal's own labels,
        so it keeps working if tpg renames a column.
      
            
      
            
      
            
        Boardings are counted by sensors above the vehicle doors and cleaned up by tpg afterwards — which is
        why they come out with decimals. Stops that tpg counts but that aren't in the stop reference (or that
        moved) can't be drawn on the map; they're still searchable.
