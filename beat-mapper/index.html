Slice samples

Every transient from step 1 starts a slice, running to the next transient (plus a tail) or for a fixed length. Switch the loop on and only what is inside it is sliced
Transients closer together than this are skipped, so the slice before them keeps running
What each .wav gets: click-free fades, per-slice normalizing to the target dBFS, source channels or a mono mix, 16 or 24-bit at the file's sample rate
Play the selected slice on its own, exactly as it will be written · a slice you click or step onto plays tooE
